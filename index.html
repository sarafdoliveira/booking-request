Good Together: Care Package Social

Meet new people while doing something good. Visit.org brings groups together for an evening of packing care packages for neighbors facing food insecurity, followed by a social hour with tasty treats. Every package your team assembles goes straight to a partner food bank in your city, and part of every booking funds the next round of supplies.

In partnership with Common Table Food Network

In-person · Team building • Typically 2 hours • 10-40 Participants • EN, ES +1Also available in Portuguese

Details

Your group arrives at a partner space in Chicago, Miami, New York, Los Angeles, Dallas, Boston, Atlanta, Austin or Denver. After a short welcome from your Visit.org host, you'll split into packing crews and work through an assembly line of fresh produce, pantry staples and hygiene items.

Each crew finishes its boxes with handwritten notes of encouragement. Once the last box is sealed, the room turns into a social hour with drinks, snacks and time to get to know the people you just worked beside.

    Meet the community

    You'll pack alongside local community volunteers and other teams booked the same evening. Expect a friendly mix of colleagues, new neighbors and first-time volunteers.

    What's included

    • All packing supplies, food and hygiene items for 3 care packages per participant
    • A social hour with drinks and tasty treats from a local bakery
    • A post-event impact report with packages delivered and households reached

    What to bring

    • Comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes for the packing line
    • A reusable water bottle
    • Your best handwriting for the encouragement notes

    What else you should know

    The venue is wheelchair accessible and the packing line can be set up at seated height on request. Snacks include vegetarian and gluten-free options; tell us about other dietary needs in your booking request. Care packages are collected by the partner food bank the next morning, so nothing needs to be carried home. Requests need at least 14 days' notice so the host can source supplies.

    Common Table Food Network
    Nonprofit · 9 US cities

    Common Table connects surplus food from grocers and farms with neighborhood pantries, schools and shelters. Its local teams run distribution in nine cities and rely on corporate and community volunteers to sort, pack and deliver food to families who need it. Every care package assembled at a Good Together event is logged in the network's delivery system, so your team's impact report shows exactly where the boxes went. In 2025 the network delivered more than 1.2 million meals and recruited 18,000 first-time volunteers.
